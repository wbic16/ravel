## 2026-11-26 — dedup pass: fan-out dedup hash-priced, full-scan floor beaten

- Will's direction (2026-11-26): "do it now" — the pass the optimization entry above named as the remaining floor. `phext/serve/src/sparql.rs`: `match_bgp`'s fan-out dedup (defect #104's discipline) membership-tested each extension against a `Vec<Binding>` — quadratic on all-variable patterns. Membership is now answered from a `HashSet` of `BindingKey`, the canonical hashable key for a binding: its `(variable, term)` pairs sorted ascending by variable name. Key equality is exactly binding equality (the sorted pair list is a canonical form of the map's contents, not a digest), total over partial bindings (unbound = absent). Extensions still collect in first-occurrence order; the extensions kept, and their order, are unchanged.
- Attribution before the fix, three ways: subset scaling ×4.1 per doubling (minima 188 / 773 / 3,190 ms at 3,762 / 7,523 / 15,046 quads); a perf profile dominated by `HashMap` equality under the membership scan (61.7% of samples); and ablation of the membership test alone (throwaway build) taking the count 5,486 → 15.2 ms — the test was ~99.7% of the full-scan cost. Sibling audit found no other data-scaled quadratic membership in the evaluator (projection DISTINCT, output dedup, and cross-graph dedup already hash; the GRAPH/OPTIONAL fan-outs carry no membership tests).
- Results (state soul, in-process medians): `GRAPH ?g` COUNT 5,486 → 46.5 ms; raw scan 4,603 → 71.9 ms; fan-out shape 244.8 → 4.7 ms; full-path count via probe 3,966 → 54.5 ms. Point path unchanged (evaluator 0.0018 → 0.0019 ms; full-path 0.355 → 0.360 ms). Equivalence proven by differential output digests over a 12-query battery — byte-identical before/after, including the 15,046-row scan and the fan-out shape. Suites: crate 30/30 (2 new tests: the key's equality contract, dedup exactness at 400-subject scale), smokes 30/30 + 15/15, harness 72/72 both transports, Wavelets 19/19 both. Full record: the dedup-pass section of `phext/OPTIMIZATION.md`.
